Ingredients

  • Wheat flour
  • Sugar
  • Ghee

Steps & Emotions

  1. Knead dough - grounding myself
  2. Roll softly - calming thoughts
  3. Add sugar - small joy
  4. Fold layers - holding emotions
  5. Cook slowly - warmth returns
  6. Flip gently - patience
  7. Add ghee - comfort spreads
  8. Eat warm - peace settles
