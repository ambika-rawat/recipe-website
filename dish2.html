SANNA NIMBU

"A winter taste from the mountains."

Home CHINNI KA PARATHA SANNA NIMBU DOODH BHATH BURANSH JUICE WHITE SAUCEPASTA End Note
"Cold weather, warm memories."

Ingredients

  • Lemon
  • Salt
  • Spices
  • Mustard oil

Steps & Emotions

  1. Cut lemons and seperate the inner part into smaller pieces - sharp freshness
  2. Add special pahadi (roasted cumin seeds,green chillies,bhang,salt) - reality hits
  3. Add spices - life gets bold
  4. Mix well with curd - blending moods
  5. Add fruits or salad as per your taste ( radish,pomogrenate,guavas are more common) - patience builds
  6. Smell rises - nostalgia
  7. You can Serve with musturd oil too but I personally prefer it without it- warmth spreads
  8. Taste - feels like home

Time: 10 mins | Difficulty: Easy
